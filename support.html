Frequently Asked Questions

Why are prayer notification alerts delayed or missing on Android?

Modern Android operating systems enforce aggressive battery saver modes that may silence background alarm timers. To guarantee exact Adhan alerts on time:

  • Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Muslim Ultra.
  • Select Battery and choose "Unrestricted" (or disable Battery Optimization).
  • Ensure "Allow Alarms & Reminders" permission is enabled.

How do I calibrate the Qibla Compass?

The Qibla compass relies on your smartphone's built-in magnetometer. If the dial appears inaccurate or wanders:

  • Step away from large magnetic sources (laptops, metal desks, magnetic phone cases).
  • Hold your phone flat and gently wave it in a figure-8 motion for 5–10 seconds.
  • Ensure location accuracy / high precision GPS mode is turned on.

How do I change calculation methods (MWL, ISNA, Umm al-Qura, Karachi)?

Navigate to Settings → Prayer Calculation Method inside the app. You can switch between all standard international conventions (Muslim World League, Islamic Society of North America, Egyptian General Authority, Umm Al-Qura University, University of Islamic Sciences Karachi, and more) as well as adjust Asr Juristic Method (Standard Shafi/Maliki/Hanbali or Hanafi).

Can I listen to Quran audio offline without internet?

Yes. When playing any Surah or verse, tap the Download icon to save the reciter's audio files locally to your device storage. Once cached, recitations play instantly without requiring data or Wi-Fi.

Is my personal location data ever shared or tracked?

Never. Muslim Ultra calculates all solar prayer angles and Qibla bearings strictly on your physical device. We maintain no location tracking databases and run zero behavioral analytics.
