Frequently Asked Questions

Why are prayer notification alerts delayed or missing on Android?

Modern Android versions enforce aggressive battery optimization that can silence background alarm timers. To guarantee exact Adhan alerts:

  • Open Settings → Apps → Muslim Ultra on your phone.
  • Tap Battery and select "Unrestricted".
  • Ensure "Allow Alarms & Reminders" permission is enabled.
How do I calibrate the Qibla Compass?

The compass uses your smartphone's built-in magnetometer. If the bearing drifts:

  • Step away from large magnetic sources (laptops, metal frames, magnetic phone cases).
  • Hold your phone flat and wave it in a smooth figure-8 motion for 5–10 seconds.
  • Ensure location precision GPS mode is turned on.
How do I change calculation conventions (MWL, ISNA, Umm al-Qura)?

Open Settings → Prayer Calculation Method inside Muslim Ultra. You can switch between all standard authorities (Muslim World League, ISNA, Makkah Umm al-Qura, Egyptian Authority, and Karachi) and adjust Asr Juristic Method (Standard Shafi/Maliki/Hanbali or Hanafi).

Can I listen to Quran recitations offline?

Yes. Tap the download icon next to any Surah to save the high-quality EveryAyah recitations locally on your device for instant offline playback anytime without data.

Is my location or prayer history ever sent to the cloud?

Never. Muslim Ultra calculates all solar prayer angles and Qibla bearings strictly on your physical device. We maintain zero tracking databases and zero telemetry collectors.
