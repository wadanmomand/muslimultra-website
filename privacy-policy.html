Privacy Policy

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

Last updated: October 7, 2026 • Muslim Ultra (com.muslimultra.app)

1. Core Privacy Principles

Muslim Ultra is engineered from the ground up as a privacy-first, tracker-free spiritual companion. We believe your worship, location, and queries are sacred and belong exclusively to you.

The Zero-Tracker Guarantee

Muslim Ultra contains zero third-party trackers, zero advertising SDKs, and zero crash telemetry collectors (no Google Analytics, no Firebase Analytics, no Facebook SDK). Your usage data is never packaged, sold, or shared with data brokers.

2. Location & Prayer Time Calculation

To calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction, Muslim Ultra requires access to your device's geographical coordinates:

  • 100% On-Device Processing: All astronomical solar algorithms and Qibla trigonometry calculations execute locally on your physical device.
  • Zero Location Logging: Your exact GPS coordinates and location history are never transmitted to our servers or stored in remote databases.

3. Deen Companion (AI Assistant)

When you ask questions in the Deen Companion assistant:

  • Encrypted Transport: Questions are securely transmitted over HTTPS with TLS 1.3 to our edge gateway.
  • Corpus Grounding: Inquiries are matched against authentic Islamic sources (Quran, Sahih Hadith, and scholarly texts).
  • 14-Day Ephemeral Cache: To protect system integrity and enforce fair usage rate limits, question hashes and responses are temporarily cached in an encrypted cache for up to 14 days, after which they are automatically purged.
  • No Identity Association: Queries are processed under anonymous sessions and are not linked to your personal identity or email.

4. Quran Audio & Font Assets

To deliver offline-first audio recitation and beautiful Arabic calligraphy typography:

  • Audio files are fetched directly from public CDN endpoints (such as EveryAyah) and cached locally on your device for offline playback.
  • Uthmani font bundles are bundled within the app or downloaded once, generating no behavioral tracking footprint.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because Muslim Ultra operates with local storage for your bookmarks, streaks, notes, and preferences, all personal progress data resides solely on your physical device. You can erase all app data at any time through your device application settings.

6. Contact & Data Inquiries

For any questions, clarifications, or audit inquiries regarding our privacy practices, contact our security and engineering team directly:
