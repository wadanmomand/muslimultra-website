About Muslim Ultra

A privacy-first Islamic companion — for your prayers, your Quran, and your learning.

Our Mission

Muslim Ultra exists for one reason: to help Muslims worship and learn with confidence, authenticity, and complete privacy. Too many Islamic apps today monetize your data — tracking your location, your prayers, and your questions. We built Muslim Ultra as the opposite: a companion that serves you without surveilling you.

Every design decision — from on-device prayer calculations to a tracker-free codebase — flows from a simple belief: your ibadah is sacred, and your data belongs to you alone.

What We Build

  • The Muslim Ultra app — accurate prayer times, a tilt-compensated Qibla compass, a Tanzil-based Quran reader with translations, daily duas, and Muslim AI, an assistant that answers Islamic questions with authentic citations from Quran and hadith.
  • The Muslim Ultra Quran Academy — live 1-on-1 online classes for kids and adults: Noorani Qaida, Tajweed, Hifz, and Islamic studies with qualified teachers. Every new student begins with a 3-day free trial.

Privacy Is Not a Feature. It's the Foundation.

Muslim Ultra ships with zero third-party trackers, zero advertising SDKs, and zero analytics collectors. Prayer times and Qibla direction are computed entirely on your device — your location never leaves your phone. Read the full details in our Privacy Policy.

Authenticity Above All

Religious content carries a trust that we take seriously:

  • Quranic text is sourced from the Tanzil project with verified translations.
  • Muslim AI is grounded in a curated corpus of Quran, hadith, and authentic duas — and it declines to answer rather than fabricate a reference.
  • AI answers are educational, not fatwas — we always encourage verifying rulings with qualified scholars (see our Terms of Service).
  • Academy teachers are qualified instructors, matched to each student's age and level.

Our Values

  • 🕌 Authenticity — verified sources, honest citations, no fabrication.
  • 🔒 Privacy — no trackers, no ads, no data selling. Ever.
  • 🤝 Trust — transparent policies, clear pricing, respectful conduct.
  • 📖 Knowledge — making Quranic learning accessible to every home.
